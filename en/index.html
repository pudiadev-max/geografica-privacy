Privacy policy — GeoGrafica

Last updated: 2 October 2026 Controller: Pudia Contact: pudia.dev@gmail.com

GeoGrafica is an Android geography quiz app (flags, countries, cities, monuments, neighbourhoods) that works entirely on your device. This policy explains how the app handles data. In short: GeoGrafica does not collect, send or share any personal data.

No data collection

The app does not have the Internet permission and makes no network communication. It needs no account, does not identify you and requires no permission (location, camera, contacts, storage). It contains no ads, analytics, purchases, SDKs or third-party libraries that collect data.

Data stored only on your device

The app stores on the device only:

  • your game records (best scores and games played, per level and difficulty);
  • which places you got wrong most often and which information cards you have already read, to bring back what you need to review;
  • your settings: theme, language and whether hint prices are shown.

This data stays on the phone and is accessible only to the app. Android's automatic backups are turned off (allowBackup=false): your progress does not end up in cloud backups. You can erase your progress at any time in Settings > Reset progress.

External links

The "Credits and licences" and Settings screens contain links to web pages (for example OpenStreetMap, GitHub, Wikimedia Commons, this policy, the Play Store). If you tap one, it opens in your phone's browser or in another app: that is your action, the app sends nothing and does not know what you do next. What happens on those sites follows their own policies.

Third-party content

Maps, flags, texts and photos are embedded in the app and are not downloaded while you play. Sources and licences (Natural Earth, OpenStreetMap, Wikipedia, Wikidata, Wikimedia Commons and others) are listed in the app under "Credits and licences".

Retention and deletion

Local data stays until you uninstall the app, clear its data from Android settings, or reset your progress in Settings: in every case it is all removed.

Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

Any updates will be published at this same address, with the revision date updated at the top.

Contact

For questions about this policy: pudia.dev@gmail.com.
